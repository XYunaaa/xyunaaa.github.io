<span class="anchor" id="about"></span>

I am a Ph.D. student in the FSM Lab at [City University of Hong Kong](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/), advised by Prof. [Jianping Wang](https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/~jianwang/).

My research focuses on **scene understanding, 4D reconstruction, and autonomous driving**, including multi-object tracking, sensor fusion, and reconstruction of dynamic driving scenes.

I received my master's degree with Distinction from Beihang University in June 2022. Before starting my Ph.D., I worked on LiDAR perception at Momenta.

## Education & Experience
{: #education}

- **2024 – Present**, Ph.D. in Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong.
- **2022 – 2023**, Senior Perception Algorithm Engineer, Momenta (LiDAR Perception).
- **2019 – 2022**, M.Sc. in Computer Technology, Beihang University (Distinction).

### Geometric Obstacle Detection · Momenta

Built a 33k-frame point-cloud dataset and improved detection AP from 60.8 to 71.2.

![Examples of geometric obstacle detection from point clouds](/research/Obstacle.png){: .project-image loading="lazy"}

### Dynamic Object Detection Optimization · Momenta

Built a closed-loop process for collecting problems, accumulating data, and optimizing models. Contributed more than 12k lines of code processing over 10 million data records; improved processing speed by 40× and reduced optimization time from two months to two weeks.

![Data processing and model optimization pipeline](/research/offline_filter.png){: .project-image loading="lazy"}

### Open-World Multi-Object Tracking Dataset

Collected and annotated 7,680 frames with point clouds, images, 2D and 3D detection labels, and tracking IDs, supported by a fast annotation algorithm.

![Point clouds, images, and labels in the multi-object tracking dataset](/research/dataset.png){: .project-image loading="lazy"}
